What's new

SharkyClips updates itself: Settings → Updates. Here is what changed in the latest versions.

Version 1.2.742026-11-27

One logo everywhere, a new website in 11 languages, and a simple way to send feedback. The TikTok helper can now also come from a browser store, and dialogs no longer open off-screen.

New

  • Send feedback / Report a bug. In Settings → About, and as a "Report" button on every error message. It opens a GitHub form with the app version and your system filled in, and nothing else. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
  • The SharkyClips Helper from a store. If you install the Helper from Microsoft Edge Add-ons or Firefox Add-ons (coming soon), connect it once: the Helper guide shows a 6-digit code, and you type it into the Helper's menu. With it, Firefox can fill in your upload too.
  • A new website in 11 languages: what SharkyClips does, screenshots and a short demo, download help (also for the blue "Windows protected your PC" message), questions and answers, and the update notes.

Improved

  • One logo everywhere. The app icon, the icon next to the clock, the installer pictures, the start screen, the end of a photo slideshow and the website now all use the Classic look of the app.
  • The Helper has a small menu in the browser toolbar that shows if it is connected to SharkyClips.
  • The Helper twice in one browser (for example SharkyClips' own copy and a store copy) no longer fills in the page twice: only one of them does.

Fixed

  • Dialogs on a scrolled page opened off-screen, partly under the top bar (for example the Helper guide near the bottom of Settings). They now always open in the middle of the window.
  • A choice in Settings was cut off on phones in Russian (TikTok's "Semi-automatic"). Very long words now wrap inside the button.

Version 1.2.732026-11-27

A small fix for the new Photo slideshow in the Library.

Fixed

  • A slideshow's picture in the Library was too tall and spilled out of its small box. It now fits, like every other clip.
  • The project link of a slideshow (in the Library and on the Share screen) now opens the slideshow, not an empty project page.

Version 1.2.722026-11-27

New: Photo slideshow. Turn 2 to 20 photos (for example a job from before to after) into a vertical video for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Show details

New

  • Photo slideshow, in Easy and Pro mode, with its own button on the home screen. Drop 2 to 20 photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP). They are put in order by the date they were taken, and you can drag them into a different order.
  • A clean 9:16 video: each photo shows for 3 seconds (you can pick 2 to 5), with a soft change between photos (crossfade, slide or zoom) and a slow, gentle zoom that never cuts off the important part. Photos taken sideways are turned the right way, and wide photos fit inside the frame over a blurred copy of themselves, never stretched.
  • Short text on photos, like "BEFORE", "DAY 3" or "RESULT". One switch adds Before / Progress / Result in your language. The text stays where the app buttons don't cover it.
  • An animated SharkyClips logo at the end (about 1.5 seconds). It is on by default and you can turn it off.
  • Preview, then export on your graphics card or on the processor, so it also works on slower PCs. After the export: Save, Open folder, or Share to YouTube Shorts, Instagram or TikTok with a title and description in your style. No music is added: you add a song in Instagram or YouTube.

Version 1.2.712026-11-27

A small fix: two posts going out at the same moment no longer trip over each other when they save your account sign-in.

Show details

Fixed

  • TikTok or Instagram could fail on Windows when two posts saved the account sign-in at the same moment. SharkyClips now waits a moment and tries again.

Version 1.2.702026-11-26

SharkyClips now fills in your TikTok upload in your own browser (video, caption and settings), and you check it and click Post yourself. Sharing also never gets stuck on "Sharing…" any more.

Show details

New

  • Assisted upload for TikTok (the new default): click Share and SharkyClips opens TikTok Studio in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera GX or Brave), adds the video, writes the caption with your hashtags, picks "Everyone" and "Now", and stops. A banner in the page and in the app says: "Everything is ready. Check and click Post." SharkyClips never clicks Post for you.
  • "Shared to TikTok ✓" with the link as soon as you post. If SharkyClips can't tell, it asks once: "Did you post it? Yes / No".
  • Log in only once in the SharkyClips browser window. SharkyClips never sees your password. If TikTok shows a check, SharkyClips asks you to finish it in the browser and waits.
  • Several clips in a row: the next clip opens only after you have posted the one before, with a short pause between posts.
  • Instagram Reels the same way, as an option in Settings.
  • A small helper for your browser does the filling in. Edge, Opera GX and Brave get it by themselves. Chrome needs a short setup once, and SharkyClips shows you every step.
  • Publish queue: "Cancel everything pending" and a new "cancelled" status.

Improved

  • If the TikTok page changes and SharkyClips can't fill it in, you get the by-hand steps at once (copy buttons, upload page, folder) with the reason. Posting fully by hand is still one click away in Settings.
  • Firefox: the upload page opens there and you post by hand, because Firefox can't be filled in.
  • Google's user limit: when Google stops new sign-ins for the shared app, SharkyClips explains it clearly and takes you to the steps for your own free Google project, including the "Publish app" click.

Fixed

  • "Sharing…" could stay forever while a swear in the clip was still heard. The clip now says why it waits.
  • A clip that couldn't be made now shows an error with Retry after one minute, instead of waiting forever.
  • Retry always starts the upload again, or tells you clearly why it can't.
  • A platform you post by hand never holds up the others: for example, a caption that is too long for TikTok no longer stops YouTube.
  • A by-hand post that stopped while it was being prepared now shows an error with Retry.

Version 1.2.692026-11-26

The navy and gold look is back as the default, with the cleaner text from the last update. A new Personalization page lets you pick light, dark or "follow Windows", four looks, an accent colour and your own colours.

Show details

New

  • Personalization in Settings, in Easy and Pro mode: choose Light, Dark or Follow Windows.
  • Four looks: Classic (navy and gold, the default), Ocean (deep sea blue with sea-glass teal), Shark Sign (the yellow look of the last update) and High contrast.
  • Accent colour: pick one from the palette or any colour you like. It colours the buttons, the open tab, the progress bars and the highlights. You can also show it on the window title bar.
  • Your own colours: background, panels, text, secondary text, borders, buttons, links, success, warning, error, bleeped words and the caption highlight, for light and dark mode.
  • Easy to read, always: if a colour makes text hard to read, SharkyClips tells you and fixes it by itself (you can switch this off).
  • Live preview, "Reset to default", and theme files you can save and open again or share.
  • "You already used this video" now shows as soon as you drop or pick a video, before any clip is made.

Improved

  • Every screen follows your colours: Easy and Pro mode, windows, the mini game, the start-up animation and the sign-in pages.

Fixed

  • The Shark Sign look was the only look after the last update. It is now one of four, and Classic is the default again.

Version 1.2.682026-11-26

Sharing works again and is now simple: drop a video in, tick YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, press Share, and your clips go out right away. Every clip tells you clearly if it is out, with a link, or what went wrong.

Show details

New

  • Share now. All clips you pick go out right away. There is no daily limit setting and no planning for the next days any more. The only limit is what YouTube allows today, and you always see "X YouTube uploads left today".
  • More than YouTube allows today? You choose: upload the rest by hand now, put them on tomorrow, or pick a day yourself.
  • Easy mode in three steps. Drop a video, see all your Shorts in one big grid, tick the platforms and press Share. Nothing else on the screen.
  • A message for every clip. "Shared to YouTube ✓" with a link, in the app and as a Windows notification, or a clear message about what went wrong.
  • Upload manually, for every clip and every platform. SharkyClips saves the right video, shows the title, the description and the hashtags with a copy button each, and opens the upload page and the folder.
  • Upload history. A new page with every clip you shared: date, game, clip, platform and link. You can search it, and it stays after you delete a project.
  • "Did I already upload this?" SharkyClips knows every video you used, even with another name. If you import one again, it tells you when and where its clips were shared.
  • Free up space. When the clips are shared, one button deletes the stream video and the made files. It shows first how many GB you get back. Your posts and the history stay.
  • Titles in the language you want for each video.

Improved

  • Titles made for views: ALL CAPS with the right letters of your language, fitting emojis, and #Shorts on YouTube. At most 5 hashtags on Instagram.
  • Every video is checked before it goes out (9:16, length, format, size) and fixed by itself when needed.
  • TikTok goes by hand by default now, because automatic posting can stop at a TikTok check. You can still switch automatic posting on.
  • Instagram's 4-hour gap is only a warning now. You can post anyway.
  • Pro mode shares the same way as Easy mode.

Fixed

  • No more clips that wait without a word. If YouTube is not connected, today's uploads are used up, or SharkyClips is paused while you play, the clip says so.
  • A video that YouTube keeps private is now always shown as private, with a button to open it in YouTube Studio.
  • SharkyClips counted YouTube uploads with old numbers and stopped after a few a day. It now uses YouTube's current limit of 100 uploads a day.
  • If Google blocks new sign-ins for the app (its limit of 100 users), SharkyClips now says so and what you can do.

Version 1.2.672026-11-26

A small fix for Windows: when YouTube stops accepting your sign-in, SharkyClips now always asks you to connect again, even if Windows is busy with the sign-in file for a moment.

Show details

Fixed

  • "Connect again" always shows. On Windows, another program could hold the YouTube sign-in file for a moment. The upload then failed without asking you to connect again. Now SharkyClips waits a little, tries again, and always shows the "connect again" message.

Version 1.2.662026-11-22

Sharing many clips is now simple: open "Share clips", see every clip of a video at once with a clear status, tick the ones you want and share them in one go. A daily limit keeps it safe, and the rest is planned for the next days by itself.

Show details

New

  • Share clips. One big screen with all clips of a video: large pictures, play on hover or tap, length, viral score and title. Every clip says clearly if it is not shared, scheduled (with the day and time), shared (with the platform icons and a link to the live post) or failed (with a Try again button).
  • Never post the same clip twice. Shared clips are greyed out. The tabs All, Not shared and Shared help you find what is left. If you pick a clip that is already out, SharkyClips tells you and only sends it where it is not yet.
  • Pick many, share once. A box on every card, "Select all" and "Select not shared", and one big "Share selected" button. Each card also has its own Share button.
  • A daily upload limit. 5 clips a day by default, and you can choose 1 to 6 in Settings (6 is what YouTube's free limit allows). You always see how many are left, for example "3 of 5 left today". If you pick more, the rest are planned for the next days by themselves.
  • One simple flow. After Share, pick the platforms once for all clips (only the connected ones; the others have a Connect button). "Edit per clip" lets you change the title, description, hashtags and platforms of each clip. Then a summary shows what goes where and when, and how many of today's uploads it uses. Then Publish.
  • See it happen. A progress panel shows every clip of the batch and where it is on each platform.

Improved

  • Instagram stays safe. Instagram posts still keep at least 4 hours apart; the summary shows the moved times. Instagram's Manual mode and your other platform settings stay as they were.
  • Undo. Taking a clip off the plan waits a few seconds with an Undo button.
  • Your choice is remembered. The platforms you picked last time are already on next time.
  • Works the same in Easy and Pro mode, with big buttons and the keyboard: Esc clears the selection, Ctrl+A picks all clips shown.

Version 1.2.652026-11-22

SharkyClips has a new look: deep water colours, the yellow of a shark warning sign and clearer text on every screen. Every feature works exactly as before.

Show details

New

  • A new look everywhere. Every screen, window and message now uses deep sea colours with one bright yellow for the main button. The main buttons, clip cards and windows have a small cut corner, like a shark fin.
  • A new logo and app icon. The shark fin now sits on a yellow sign. The installer, the start animation and the website use the new look too.

Improved

  • Easier to read. One clear font family for the whole app, bigger and bolder headings, and times and numbers in an even-width font, so they line up.
  • Fonts work offline. The fonts are now part of the app, so it looks the same without an internet connection and loads nothing from other websites.
  • Clearer colours. Yellow now marks only the main action and the best moments. Switches that are on are green, and warnings are orange.
  • A calmer progress view. "Finding your best moments" now shows numbered steps on a line, with a bar for the step that is running.
  • Pro mode is tighter. The editor screens use less empty space, so more of your clips fit on the screen.
  • Quieter motion. Only the page itself moves when it opens; lists no longer slide in one by one.

Version 1.2.642026-11-21

A small fix for people who use a screen reader, and safer checks behind the scenes.

Show details

Fixed

  • The "Updates" link reads right. When the Updates link shows the "New" sign, screen readers now read it exactly as it looks ("Updates New").
  • More reliable checks. The automatic checks that run before each update no longer stop at the very end of a good run, so updates reach you on time.

Version 1.2.632026-11-21

Titles, descriptions and hashtags can now follow your own fixed style. Pro mode is calmer, and when you watch a clip you can always see where it ends.

Show details

New

  • My style. A new card in Settings: switch it on and every gaming title, description and hashtag list follows one fixed layout. You get 3 title options in capital letters with 1-2 emojis at the end. The description starts with a short greeting, tells what happened in a few sentences, then adds your subscribe line, your social links and the hashtags. Other languages get the same layout, translated. Your social links are always used exactly as you typed them.
  • Episode numbers for story games. For a story game (like Spider-Man 2) the long-video title gets the episode number, and the next project of the same game gets the next number. You can change the number in the project.
  • Only real facts. The texts use only what was said in the clip, what the game showed, the project name or what you typed. If a text names a character or event that is not in the clip, it is written again. When it is not sure, it stays general.
  • Write one field again. The title, the description and the hashtags each have their own "Regenerate" button now.
  • See where a clip ends. When you watch a clip, the progress bar shows the clip's start and end, the part of the clip is highlighted, and you see how many seconds are left. At the end, the clip stops exactly on its last frame (or starts again, if you choose "Loop"), and a small "End of clip" sign appears. This works in both modes, in the big full-screen preview and on every screen size.

Improved

  • A calmer Pro mode. Every Pro screen now shows only the actions you use most. The others are still there, in a closed "More options" or "Advanced" section. Settings shows the main cards first and keeps the rest under "Advanced".
  • Clearer names. "Queue" is now called "Jobs", so it is not mixed up with "Publish queue". In the Library, the YouTube-only button is now called "YouTube now".
  • Games from the project name. When the project name says Battlefield 6, Rematch or Spider-Man 2, the texts now name and tag the right game.

Fixed

  • Hidden "Updates" link. On a 1280 px wide screen the last link of the Pro top bar was cut off. All links fit now.
  • Turkish letters in titles. Words written without Turkish letters (like "MUKEMMEL") are now corrected ("MÜKEMMEL"). English names stay as they are.

Questions and answers

Can't find your question? Ask us.

Is SharkyClips really free?

Yes. It is open source (MIT license). There is no subscription, no credits, no watermark and no ads. The AI runs on your own PC, so there is nothing to pay for.

Does SharkyClips upload my videos anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your PC: speech recognition, the AI, finding moments and making clips. There is no SharkyClips server and no tracking. Your videos only leave your PC when you share a clip to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram yourself. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why do I need my own Google project for YouTube? What is the 100-user limit?

To upload to YouTube, an app needs a Google sign-in. Google limits a sign-in that it has not reviewed to 100 users in total (Google calls this the user cap). One shared sign-in for every SharkyClips user would be full very fast.

So the download has no shared Google sign-in. Instead you connect a free Google project of your own. Then the limit and the daily upload quota belong to you alone. It is free, needs no credit card and takes about 5 to 10 minutes, once.

How do I connect my own Google project?

In SharkyClips: Settings → YouTube → Connect your own Google project. A guide opens the right Google pages for you:

  1. Create a project (any name).
  2. Turn on the YouTube Data API v3.
  3. Set up the consent screen (External) and add yourself as a test user.
  4. Create an OAuth client of the type Desktop app and download its file.
  5. Drop that file into SharkyClips and click Connect with Google.

Google may say the app is not verified: that is your own project, so you can continue. While the project is in Testing, Google ends the sign-in after 7 days; set it to In production to stay signed in. Full guide.

How do I set up the TikTok helper extension?

The SharkyClips Helper is a small browser extension. It fills in your TikTok (and, if you want, Instagram) upload page and stops before Post.

  • Microsoft Edge, Opera, Opera GX, Brave: nothing to do. SharkyClips adds the Helper by itself when it opens the upload page.
  • Google Chrome: one setup step. SharkyClips shows a short guide: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder SharkyClips shows you.
  • The first time, log in to TikTok in that browser window yourself. SharkyClips never sees your password.

The Helper only runs on TikTok and Instagram pages and only talks to SharkyClips on your PC. Without it, SharkyClips copies the caption for you and you post by hand.

Will it run on my PC? I don't have a gaming PC.

Yes. You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and 8 GB RAM. A GPU is not needed. On a PC without an NVIDIA GPU, SharkyClips picks Light mode by itself: a smaller speech model and a smaller AI that run on the processor. It is slower, but it works: a 17-minute video takes about 16 minutes on a small 2-core laptop. With an NVIDIA GPU it is much faster. See the system requirements.

Does it slow down my games?

No. In the default Silent mode SharkyClips pauses its heavy work while you play or watch something fullscreen and gives the game its graphics memory back. It goes on when you stop.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That is SmartScreen warning about a new download that is not signed with a paid certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file with the .sha256 file of the release. Step by step.

Which videos can I use?

Twitch, YouTube and Kick recordings (with their chat replay), most other video sites, or any video file on your PC. Long streams of several hours are fine. Only use videos you own or have the right to use.

Does it post to TikTok and Instagram for me?

SharkyClips prepares everything and the Helper fills in the upload page, but you click Post. Instagram is always shared by you. YouTube uploads can be automatic once you connect your own Google project.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Windows is the main platform, with a one-click installer. There is a one-command installer for macOS and a manual install for Linux and developers, but they are tested less. See the install guide.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

In the app: Settings → About → Send feedback, or the same button in an error message. It opens a GitHub form with the app version and your system filled in, and nothing else. Or use the contact page.

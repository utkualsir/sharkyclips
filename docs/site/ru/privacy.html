Политика конфиденциальности

Коротко

  • SharkyClips работает на вашем компьютере. У него нет сервера, аккаунта и слежки.
  • Ваши видео, расшифровки, клипы и данные YouTube остаются на компьютере. Создатели SharkyClips ничего из этого не получают.
  • Приложение выходит в сеть только для того, что вы запускаете: загрузки, обновления и публикации на YouTube, в TikTok или Instagram.
  • YouTube необязателен. Отключите его в Настройках или уберите доступ в аккаунте Google.

Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version: SharkyClips runs on your computer. It has no server, no account and no telemetry. Your videos, transcripts, clips and YouTube data stay on your computer. The people who make SharkyClips never receive any of it.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the SharkyClips desktop app (the "app") and this website. SharkyClips is a free, open-source project published on GitHub (the "developers", "we"). You install the app on your own computer and it runs there.

2. Data the app works with

Everything below is created and stored on your computer only, in your SharkyClips projects folder (Videos\SharkyClips with the Windows installer, workspace in a manual install) and in your settings folder (~/.sharkyclips):

  • Videos and photos you import and what the app makes from them: audio, transcripts, highlight lists, rendered clips, photo slideshows, exports.
  • Chat replays of streams you import, and the live chat of your own stream if you turn on live chat reading. Messages and author names are kept on your computer to find moments where chat got excited, for "!clip" markers and to show chat bubbles in clips (links, swear words, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and @mentions are never shown). The live chat log is deleted after 14 days.
  • Live markers you set during a stream (a hotkey, "!clip" in chat): the time and who set it.
  • Your settings and preferences, such as language, title feedback (thumbs up/down), glossary, music library and brand kit.
  • View counts you enter yourself to calibrate the viral score.
  • Your voice sample and consent, only if you set up "dub in my voice": a short recording of your own voice (or a file you pick) and your typed name, in ~/.sharkyclips/voice. It is used only to speak your own lines in another language, on your computer, and never for anyone else's voice. "Forget my voice" in Settings deletes it.
  • AI cover pictures made on your computer from the AI's cover idea.

Speech recognition, the AI model, the cover image model and the voice model run locally (Whisper, Ollama, Z-Image-Turbo in stable-diffusion.cpp, Pocket TTS). The app does not send your videos, voice, transcripts or prompts to any AI service.

3. When the app goes online

Only for things you start or allow:

  • Downloading a video or its chat replay from a link you paste (Twitch, YouTube, Kick and other sites, through yt-dlp). These sites receive a normal request from your computer.
  • Downloading the AI models once (from Hugging Face, Ollama and GitHub), including the cover image model and its small program and the voice model, the first time you use them, and the parts the Windows installer sets up on the first start (for example GPU libraries).
  • Checking for and installing updates from GitHub, when you click "Check for updates".
  • The optional YouTube connection described below.
  • Checking the public pages of channels you add in Settings → Channels (Kick, Twitch, YouTube) for new streams, and downloading the recordings of those streams. No login is used.
  • Reading your Twitch chat during a stream, if you enter your channel name: a read-only, anonymous connection to Twitch's chat server. The app never posts anything.
  • Your own Telegram bot, if you connect one (phone approval): the app sends the cover picture, title and score of ready clips (and the clip itself when you tap Watch) to Telegram's Bot API and receives your button presses. Telegram processes this under its own privacy policy. The bot token is stored encrypted on your computer.
  • OBS on your own computer (its local WebSocket server), if it is turned on, to read the recording time of a marker.
  • TikTok and Instagram: by default the app opens their upload page in a SharkyClips profile of your own browser, where you log in yourself. If you added the SharkyClips Helper browser extension, it fills in the video and the caption on that page and stops; you check the post and click Post yourself. The extension only runs on tiktok.com and instagram.com pages and only talks to the SharkyClips app on your own computer (127.0.0.1); it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Without the extension the app copies the caption to your clipboard and you post by hand. Automatic posting to Instagram is switched off. If you set up automatic TikTok posting with your own developer app, the app sends the clip and its caption directly to the TikTok Content Posting API and reads back the post's status and link. TikTok and Meta process this under their own privacy policies. The access tokens (and your TikTok app's key) are stored encrypted on your computer; "Disconnect" deletes them. With TikTok the app uses user.info.basic (your open id, display name and avatar, to show which account a clip goes to), video.publish (to post the clip you set up, with the privacy and interaction settings you choose on TikTok's post screen) and video.list (views and likes of your own posts, shown next to each clip). Post ids, links and these numbers are stored in each project's platform_uploads.json.

"Send feedback" (in Settings and in error messages) opens a GitHub page in your browser with the app version and your system (for example "Windows 11") filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it there yourself, and you can change or remove anything before you do.

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.

4. YouTube data

SharkyClips uses the YouTube API Services. Connecting a YouTube channel is optional. By connecting, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google processes your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

In short:
  • What is used: the app uses your Google / YouTube data to upload the clips you choose to your own channel, and for things around those uploads: scheduling, editing or cancelling them, showing which channel is connected, and showing how those clips are doing. It never touches other videos, comments, subscribers or watch history.
  • Where it is stored: only locally on your own computer. The sign-in token is encrypted. There is no SharkyClips server, and the developers never receive your data.
  • How to stop it: press Disconnect in Settings → YouTube, or remove SharkyClips on your Google account permissions page (also reachable through Google's security settings).
  • Rules that apply: the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not read the contents of your other videos, your comments, subscribers or watch history, or other people's channels (live chat is read only for your own live stream, only when you turn it on). It only changes or deletes videos that were uploaded with SharkyClips, and only when you ask (editing or cancelling a scheduled clip).

Where it is stored

On your computer only: the access and refresh token in ~/.sharkyclips/youtube/token.enc (encrypted with Windows DPAPI or a key in the macOS Keychain), your publishing plan in ~/.sharkyclips/publish/plan.json, upload records and statistics in each project's uploads.json, and the derived learning data in ~/.sharkyclips/calibration.json and ~/.sharkyclips/hook_tests.json. The app talks directly to Google's servers (googleapis.com); there is no SharkyClips server in between.

How long it is kept

  • While YouTube is connected and the app runs, the numbers it read (views, likes, privacy, retention) are refreshed every few hours.
  • Any number that was not refreshed for 30 days is deleted automatically.
  • Disconnect deletes every number the app read from YouTube, at once. If Google ends the sign-in (for example because you removed access in your Google account) and you don't reconnect, the app deletes them after 7 days.
  • What stays: the ids of the videos you uploaded with SharkyClips (so the app knows which videos are its own) and numbers you typed in yourself.

Sharing

We do not receive, sell, rent or share YouTube data or any other data. It is not used for advertising and not transferred to anyone. SharkyClips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in Settings → YouTube revokes the token at Google, deletes it from your computer and deletes the numbers the app read from YouTube.
  • You can also revoke access at any time on your Google account permissions page (Google's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Find "SharkyClips" in the list and remove its access.
  • Calibration data can be cleared on the Stats page. Deleting a project's folder from the workspace deletes its upload records. The uninstaller offers to delete your settings folder, including all YouTube data. You can also delete the files listed above yourself at any time.

5. LAN mode

If you start the app in LAN mode, devices on your local network can open its interface. Set an access PIN or password in Settings so only you can. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash on your computer.

6. This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. It remembers your theme and language choice in your browser's local storage; that never leaves your browser. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). The fonts are served from this site itself; nothing is loaded from other servers.

7. Children

SharkyClips is not directed at children under 13. Uploading to YouTube requires a YouTube account, which has its own age requirements.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date, and the change is listed in the app's changelog.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. For anything private, use the private reporting form.
